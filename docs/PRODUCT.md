# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pandora employees record work journals and manage their tasks on Android. Managers browse authorized subordinate tasks and journals. Administrators maintain personnel and organizations through the web client.

## Product Purpose

Support task creation, approval, execution, review and work-journal retrieval with role and organization permissions enforced by the backend.

## Brand Personality

The Android interface follows the existing Pandora theme and system typography. This work emphasizes compact, readable task information and clearly separated functional panels.

## Anti-references

The confirmed task-summary brief excludes KPI dashboards, charts, large entry cards and a sixth bottom-navigation item. Avoid explanatory implementation text in normal browsing flows.

## Design Principles

- Keep the view-page entry lightweight and the summary to three functional panels.
- Preview bounded real records and provide complete lists through explicit navigation.
- Reuse existing details, avatars, labels and theme tokens.
- Preserve backend access control and show accurate loading, empty and failure states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing Compose text scaling and semantic controls. Allow content to wrap or grow rather than clipping larger text. Keep readable contrast and visible item boundaries.

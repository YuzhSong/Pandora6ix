## Why

The walkthrough needs visible refresh and future filtering affordances on Home, a clearer placement for adding today’s logs, and neutral task-management entry buttons.

- Move the today-log action below today’s entries; newly added entries appear above the action.
- Render task dispatch and review entry cards in white.
